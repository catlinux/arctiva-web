# Arctiva name and logo policy

> Versión en castellano: [TRADEMARKS.es.md](TRADEMARKS.es.md)

The Arctiva code is free software under the [GPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSE). The **Arctiva name and logo** (the files in `branding/logo/` and their variants) are not covered by that licence. They belong to CatLinux and identify the official project.

## Allowed without asking

- Referring to the project by its name and logo: articles, reviews, videos, tutorials, links and talks.
- Redistributing unmodified official ISOs and packages, with their branding.
- Saying that a product is "based on Arctiva" or "compatible with Arctiva", as long as it does not suggest it is official.

## Requires written permission from CatLinux

- Distributing a modified version (another ISO, packages or a fork) under the Arctiva name or with its logo. Modified versions must remove the branding (the `arctiva-branding` package and its references) and use a different name.
- Modifying the logo or using it as part of another brand, product or domain name.
- Using the name or logo in a way that suggests endorsement or official status.

Arctiva is based on Arch Linux but is not affiliated with or endorsed by it. "Arch Linux" and its logo are trademarks of their respective owners.
